About Ancestra AI

Ancestra AI was created to preserve more than memories, it preserves identity. While traditional family records store names, dates, and photos, they cannot hold conversations, share personal stories, or reflect how someone thought and spoke.

Ancestra AI uses ethical artificial intelligence to create digital companions that reflect a person’s personality, values, and knowledge, allowing future generations to interact with family history in a meaningful and engaging way.

How It Works

Ancestra AI builds each digital companion using a combination of personal data and direct interaction, ensuring the AI represents the individual accurately and respectfully.

With informed consent, Ancestra AI can analyse personal materials such as written text, videos, and social media content to understand speech patterns, interests, and values. This provides the foundation for modelling how the person communicates and thinks.

In addition, individuals can participate in one-on-one conversations with a dedicated chatbot. During these sessions, the AI asks questions, listens to stories, and gathers personal details, helping to form a deeper and more authentic personality model.

The AI is designed to only reflect information the individual actually knew or shared, avoiding fabricated knowledge. Over time, the digital companion can continue learning through approved interactions with family members, allowing it to grow alongside future generations.



Through this process, Ancestra AI transforms family history into a living, interactive legacy that connects generations across time.
